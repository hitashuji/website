Devta Chaturmukh Mandir

Shathala, Himachal Pradesh · Completed 2011

Brief

A small shrine for Devta Chaturmukh in the village of Shathala: a carved timber porch under a steep slate roof, set against a dry-stone wall above the valley.

About the project

The porch follows the temple-building tradition of the upper Shimla hills — timber posts and beams, carved brackets, a deep gable finished with a hand-carved finial, and a slate roof pitched for the snow.

The design was worked out in a simple massing model first, to settle how the porch meets the existing stone wall and the village steps, before the woodwork was carved and raised on site.
